- `surface-container-highest`: `#36343a`

## ✒️ Typography

- **Headline Font**: `Hanken Grotesk` (weights: 500, 600, 700, 800)
- **Body & Label Font**: `Inter` (weights: 400, 500, 600, 700)

---

## 📐 Shapes & Glassmorphism
- **Primary Cards**: `rounded-2xl` (24px corner radius) with `backdrop-blur-md` (16-20px blur) and `1px` subtle champagne/rose border (`rgba(232, 180, 184, 0.15)`).
- **Buttons**: Full pill radius (`rounded-full`) with soft rose gold gradient and active glowing bloom.
- **Bottom Navigation / Header**: Floating glassmorphic bar.
